## Julia packages used

MiniDOAS.jl needs only Julia's standard libraries. Its optional extensions use (but do not
include) these packages, each under its own license:
[NCDatasets.jl](https://github.com/Alexander-Barth/NCDatasets.jl) (MIT),
[HTTP.jl](https://github.com/JuliaWeb/HTTP.jl) (MIT), [JSON3.jl](https://github.com/quinnj/JSON3.jl)
(MIT) and [Makie.jl](https://github.com/MakieOrg/Makie.jl) (MIT). NetCDF and HDF5 binaries are
provided by the Julia `*_jll` packages NCDatasets depends on.

## Example data

**Synthetic data.** The synthetic example and test data (`examples/synthetic_data.jl`,
`examples/fit_from_julia.jl`) are
generated by code and are entirely synthetic, including the cross sections. They are not
laboratory data and are not measurements.

**Real spectra.** `examples/ace_maxdoas/data/` contains 145 spectra adapted from

> Benavent, N., Garcia-Nieto, D., Cuevas, C. A. and Saiz-Lopez, A. (2020). *Raw spectra
> measurements of scattered sunlight collected using a MAX-DOAS (Multi-Axis Differential
> Optical Absorption Spectroscopy) instrument in the austral summer of 2016/17 during the
> Antarctic Circumnavigation Expedition (ACE).* Version 1.0 [Data set]. Zenodo.
> <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3827443>

licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>). Changes: the 19 fibre columns of each
spectrum were summed, the spectra were rewritten in the MFC STD format with header
information taken from the data set's LiveInfo and GPS files (the positions converted from
degrees and minutes to decimal degrees), and the wavelength calibration
was written to a separate file (details in `examples/ace_maxdoas/README.md`; the conversion
is `examples/ace_maxdoas/tools/fetch_ace_spectra.py`). The data set's authors are not
associated with MiniDOAS.jl and do not endorse it. These files are covered by CC BY 4.0, not by
MiniDOAS.jl's BSD license.

**Cross sections and solar spectrum.** `examples/ace_maxdoas/prepare.jl` downloads published
laboratory cross sections (Vandaele et al. 1998; Serdyuchenko et al. 2014; Thalman and
Volkamer 2013; Fleischmann et al. 2004; Meller and Moortgat 2000) from the MPI-Mainz UV/VIS
Spectral Atlas (Keller-Rudek et al. 2013) and the solar spectrum of Chance and Kurucz (2010)
from the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics at run time. They are not included in or
redistributed with MiniDOAS.jl; please cite them as listed in `examples/ace_maxdoas/README.md`
when you use them.

**Ring spectrum.** The rotational Raman line parameters in `src/raman_tables.jl` and the Ring
algorithm of `ring_spectrum` (`src/tools.jl`) are taken from QDOAS (`engine/ring.c`, `mediator/mediate_xsconv.c`, after Chance and Spurr, Appl. Opt. 36,
5224, 1997) and are covered by the QDOAS notice above.
