The spectra in this folder are adapted from:

  Benavent, N., Garcia-Nieto, D., Cuevas, C. A. and Saiz-Lopez, A. (2020). Raw spectra
  measurements of scattered sunlight collected using a MAX-DOAS (Multi-Axis Differential
  Optical Absorption Spectroscopy) instrument in the austral summer of 2016/17 during the
  Antarctic Circumnavigation Expedition (ACE). Version 1.0 [Data set]. Zenodo.
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3827443

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the 19 optical-fibre columns of each spectrum were summed into one spectrum; the
spectra were rewritten in the MFC STD text format with header information (times, elevation
angle, solar zenith angle, scans, exposure time) from the data set's LiveInfo files and the
ship's position from its GPS files (converted from degrees and minutes to decimal degrees); the per-pixel wavelengths were written once to
calibration/ace_uv_instrument.clb. The counts are unchanged. The conversion is
../tools/fetch_ace_spectra.py; see ../README.md.

The data set's authors are not associated with MiniDOAS.jl and do not endorse it.
